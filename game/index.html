THE GILDED HOUR

A STEALTH GAME IN THE MERIDIAN GALLERY · ONE NIGHT · ONE STAR

Midnight in the Meridian Gallery. The Meridian Star — a diamond the size of a robin's egg — sleeps on its plinth in the Vault, behind three patrols you can learn by heart. You are Wren, a thief in a borrowed cloak. Slip past Sgt. Marlowe, Pip, Odo and Bram, lift the Star and whatever else gleams, and vanish through the skylight before dawn. Shadows hide you. Lamp light betrays you. A tossed coin buys you ten seconds.

WASD / Arrows — move   SHIFT — sneak (slow, silent, harder to spot)
CLICK — toss a coin, the nearest guard goes to look   E — grab loot / the Star
P — pause   M — mute   R — retry after being caught
Vision cones: yellow calm · orange suspicious · red hunting
Original game — no commercial names, art or music. Sound is synthesised live; the game plays fully offline.
